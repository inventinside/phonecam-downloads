PhoneCam Focus privacy policy

Updated November 6, 2026.

PhoneCam Focus is published by Flower Power Design. It connects your Android device to your paired Windows PC over Wi-Fi or USB.

Data used by the app

PhoneCam uses the Android camera to scan your PC’s pairing code and to send live video when you start camera sharing. Tablet mode displays your PC’s screen on Android and sends your pen, touch, or trackpad input back to Windows. Camera streams and captures use an authenticated, encrypted connection over Wi-Fi or USB. Tablet mode uses an authenticated, encrypted connection over Wi-Fi and your device’s authorized USB debugging connection over USB.

To find and verify your paired PC over Wi-Fi, PhoneCam sends the phone model, an app-specific certificate fingerprint, and a pairing identifier. The discovery message is authenticated and readable on your local network. PhoneCam uses these details for discovery and connection security.

When you take a photo, PhoneCam saves a JPEG on your phone and sends the original to your paired PC. When you start a video recording and allow microphone access, PhoneCam uses your microphone to include sound. It records the file on the phone and transfers the original to the paired PC. Streams and captures travel directly between your devices. PhoneCam includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. Windows apps you choose to use with the virtual webcam may handle its video according to their own settings and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

On Android 10 and later, the phone saves photos in the PhoneCam album. On older Android versions, it uses the app’s pictures folder. Pending transfers stay in the app’s private storage. The phone removes the queued video after Windows confirms the file was saved. You can delete photos and videos from their save locations on your devices.

PhoneCam stores pairing credentials and preferences locally until you replace or clear them. Clearing the Android app’s storage or uninstalling it removes its credentials, preferences, and private pending transfers. The Windows companion keeps its own pairing settings and any files already saved on your PC.
