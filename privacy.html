PhoneCam Focus privacy policy

PhoneCam Focus is published by Flower Power Design. It connects your Android device to your Windows PC on your local network.

Data used by the app

The Android app uses the camera to scan your PC’s pairing code and to send live video when you start camera sharing. Tablet mode receives your PC display and returns pen and touch input. The paired devices authenticate the connection and encrypt the stream.

To find and verify your paired PC, PhoneCam sends the phone model, an app-specific certificate fingerprint, and a pairing identifier over your local network. The discovery message is authenticated and readable on that network. PhoneCam uses these details for pairing and connection security.

PhoneCam Focus does not send these streams to Flower Power Design, create recordings, or use advertising or analytics SDKs. Windows apps you choose to use with the virtual webcam may handle its video according to their own settings and policies.

Retention and deletion

PhoneCam stores pairing credentials and preferences on your devices until you replace or clear them. To remove the saved pairing and settings from your phone, clear PhoneCam Focus’s app storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. The Windows companion keeps its own pairing settings on your PC.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer email under App support on the PhoneCam listing in Google Play.
